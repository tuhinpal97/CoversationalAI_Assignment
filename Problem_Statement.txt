Experiential Learning allows professionals to blend academic theory with workplace application, involving group studies and practical projects.

Key Principles of  Experiential Learning:

Simulation: Addresses the limitations of real-world experience by allowing students to test scenarios and learn from virtual failures.
Collaboration: Encourages team-based learning through group studies and projects.
Mentorship: Involves industry professionals in the learning process.
